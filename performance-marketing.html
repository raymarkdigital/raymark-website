Common questions

What budget do I need?

You can start small. We recommend a test budget first and scale it once we see results.

When will I see results?

Google Ads can bring enquiries within days. Meta Ads usually need two to three weeks of testing.

Do I pay Google and Meta separately?

Yes. Your ad budget is paid to the platform directly and our fee is separate, so you see every rupee.

How much do your services cost?

It depends on the services and goals. We offer monthly plans, one-time audits and project pricing, with no hidden costs.

Can I start with only one service?

Yes. Many clients begin with SEO, Google Ads or social media and add more later.
